The same code that drives a cardboard robot on a Raspberry Pi, running in your browser. The sequences below are the robot's real default_sequences.json; the motion easing, the arm gestures and the party beat are ports of the Python it actually runs. No hardware, no API key, nothing to install.

idle

Motion sequences

Loaded from the robot's own sequence file. Each step interpolates over move_time seconds, then holds — the same 20 ms easing loop as servo.py.

Speech-synced gestures

the interesting bit

Type a sentence. The arms are not animated randomly — a keyframe timeline is derived from the words themselves: one beat per word, bigger for longer words, both arms thrown on ! and ?, relaxing to rest at each full stop. Try "Wow! Really? Yes, absolutely incredible."

The fun ones

Party mode plays a dance sequence over a beat this page synthesizes from scratch. Face tracking asks for your camera and pans the head to follow you — the video never leaves your device.
